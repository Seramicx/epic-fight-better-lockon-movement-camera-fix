## 1.2.1

- **Iron's Spells Integration**: Character now smoothly auto-faces the enemy when casting spells while locked on (works for continuous/held spells and instant casts).
- **Adaptive Crosshair Improvements**: The third-person adaptive crosshair now strictly follows the player's true aim point when drawing bows, crossbows, tridents, or channeling spells.
- **Camera Snapping for Instant Spells**: When you tap to cast an instant spell (like Teleport or Firebolt) in third person, your character instantly snaps to aim exactly where the crosshair is pointing, ignoring the shoulder camera offset. 
- Over-the-shoulder camera offset tweaked for better default alignment.
- Removed some leftover adaptive body aim code that was interfering with the crosshair's parallax.

**Minecraft:** 1.20.1 Forge. **Requires:** Epic Fight (Better Lock On optional).
